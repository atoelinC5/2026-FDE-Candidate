核心職責
- AI 數位小隊調度與產出整合：熟練調度 AI Agents 與自動化工作流，將多元片段素材精煉整合為企業級高質量成果。
- AI 幻覺防禦與品質把關：運用領域知識與批判性思考審查 AI 產出，消除邏輯漏洞與 AI 幻覺，確保商業與技術極高準確性。

跨領域前線部署：
- 產品管理 (PM)：運用 AI 工具快速進行市場洞察、用戶痛點分析與產品規格定義。
- 市場與增長 (Marketing & Growth)：透過數據與 AI 工具驗證市場假設，推動高效增長策略與商業包裝。
- 技術服務與支援 (FAE)：解構客戶技術痛點，快速建構 PoC 原型，架起商業願景與工程落地的橋樑。

工作邊界之持續重塑：主動內化最前沿 AI 科技與工程，持續優化團隊工作流並突破價值極限。

應徵條件與發展
 - 非典型跨領域專業背景：具備 PM、市場策略與技術支援實務經驗，能於高模糊度環境中流暢切換角色。
 - 卓越 AI 素養與工作流設計能力：精通 LLMs、Prompt 工程、AI Agent 框架及自動化工具，具 AI 工作流實作經驗。
 - 強大決策力與批判性思維：敏銳辨識 AI 產出瑕疵，將粗糙素材轉化為高品質商業決策。

高度適應力與自我演進能力：具強大自主學習力，樂於隨技術演進持續重塑自身職責。

Job Title: Forward-Deployed AI Architect

Role Overview
  We are seeking a high-leverage Forward-Deployed AI Architect. As AI drives a fundamental paradigm shift, exceptional talent should not be constrained by traditional, siloed corporate functions (PM, Marketing, FAE). As a Tactical Leader commanding an AI-powered squad, you will operate at the intersection of product strategy, market enablement, and technical solutioning to exponentially amplify execution. Your core value lies in strategic decision-making, synthesizing cross-disciplinary outputs, and exercising rigorous quality control—specifically mitigating AI hallucinations to ensure enterprise-grade accuracy.

Core Responsibilities
- AI Squad Orchestration & Synthesis: Command and orchestrate AI agents and automated workflows. Synthesize complex AI-generated outputs into high-precision, enterprise-ready deliverables.
- Guardianship Against AI Hallucinations: Apply deep domain expertise and critical thinking to audit AI outputs, eliminating logic flaws and hallucinations to ensure absolute technical and contextual accuracy.
- Cross-Functional Forward Deployment:
    Product Management (PM): Accelerate market research and product scoping via AI frameworks.
    Marketing & Growth: Validate market hypotheses through data-driven AI capabilities to drive high-impact go-to-market strategies.
    Field Application Engineering (FAE): Interface with complex client requirements, rapidly develop PoC prototypes, and bridge commercial vision with engineering execution.
- Continuous Redefinition of Scope: Actively internalize cutting-edge AI tech to continuously optimize workflows and expand value limits.
